# NIJA Mobile Privacy Policy — Counsel Review Draft

**Last updated:** August 27, 2026  
**Status:** Pre-release draft. Must be reviewed by qualified counsel and reconciled against the exact production release before publication.

NIJA AI Trading ("NIJA," "we," "our," or "us") provides software for trading education, simulation, broker-account monitoring, risk controls, and, where enabled, broker-connected trading functionality. This Privacy Policy describes the categories of information NIJA may process through the mobile application and related services, why that information is used, how it may be shared, and the choices available to users.

This draft intentionally avoids claiming regulatory status, certifications, or retention periods that have not been legally verified.

## 1. Information NIJA May Process

### Account and contact information

When you create or use a NIJA account, NIJA may process information such as:

- email address;
- NIJA user/account identifier;
- subscription or entitlement status;
- account settings and preferences;
- records of Terms of Service acceptance and risk acknowledgments.

Passwords are intended to be stored as password hashes rather than plaintext passwords.

### Broker connection information

If you choose to connect a supported brokerage or exchange, NIJA may process:

- broker or exchange name;
- API credentials or other connection credentials needed to authenticate the connection;
- broker account metadata;
- balances, positions, orders, fills, and trade history made available through the connection.

The current NIJA backend architecture supports encrypted server-side handling of user broker credentials. The mobile app must not embed broker secrets in the application package. Users should grant only the minimum broker permissions necessary for the selected feature and should disable withdrawal permissions where the broker supports permission scoping.

### Trading, simulation, and risk data

NIJA may process information generated by use of the service, including:

- simulated trading activity;
- broker-connected order and trade activity;
- position information;
- profit/loss and performance calculations;
- strategy, risk, stop, or exposure settings;
- system state and safety events;
- consent and acknowledgment timestamps.

Simulated or historical results do not guarantee future performance.

### Device, session, and security information

Depending on the release configuration, NIJA may process:

- device platform, model, operating-system version, and application version;
- device identifier used for app/device management;
- push-notification token if notifications are enabled;
- IP address;
- user agent;
- session identifiers;
- login, authentication, security, and error events.

### Purchase and subscription information

If paid mobile features are offered, Apple, Google, Stripe, or another authorized payment provider may process payment information. NIJA may receive transaction identifiers, subscription status, entitlement state, purchase dates, and related metadata needed to provide access and customer support. NIJA should not claim to receive full payment-card data unless the actual payment integration does so.

### Diagnostics and analytics

NIJA may process crash, performance, or product-interaction data only to the extent that such diagnostics or analytics are actually enabled in the release build. Before release, NIJA will identify the applicable SDKs and providers in its store privacy disclosures and vendor inventory.

## 2. How NIJA Uses Information

NIJA may use information to:

- create, authenticate, secure, and administer user accounts;
- provide education and simulation features;
- connect to a user's selected broker when authorized;
- display balances, positions, orders, trades, and system status;
- apply user-selected and server-enforced risk controls;
- process subscription entitlements;
- send requested operational or security notifications;
- provide customer support;
- detect abuse, fraud, unauthorized access, and security incidents;
- maintain service reliability and troubleshoot errors;
- comply with lawful obligations and enforce applicable agreements.

NIJA does not guarantee profits, returns, income, order fills, or trading success.

## 3. When Information May Be Shared

NIJA may transmit or disclose information to service providers only as needed for the services they perform, subject to the production architecture and applicable agreements. Categories may include:

- the brokerage or exchange you choose to connect;
- hosting, database, security, and infrastructure providers;
- Apple or Google for app distribution, billing, notifications, or related platform functions when enabled;
- payment processors when used;
- support, email, analytics, or diagnostics providers when actually integrated;
- professional advisers and authorities when disclosure is legally required or reasonably necessary to protect rights, safety, security, or prevent fraud.

The final published policy must identify or otherwise appropriately describe material production processors based on the release configuration.

## 4. Data Selling and Advertising Tracking

NIJA does not intend to sell personal information for monetary consideration. The release build must be audited for advertising, attribution, analytics, and third-party SDK behavior before NIJA makes final statements regarding "sale," "sharing," or cross-app tracking under applicable privacy laws or app-store definitions.

## 5. Security

NIJA uses administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards intended to protect information. Relevant controls include authenticated server APIs, encrypted transport, password hashing, encrypted handling of sensitive broker credentials, access controls, and server-side trading/risk authority.

No security measure is perfect. Users are responsible for protecting their devices, NIJA login credentials, and broker accounts, and should use broker API keys with the minimum permissions necessary.

## 6. Data Retention

NIJA intends to retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy and to satisfy legal, tax, accounting, security, fraud-prevention, dispute-resolution, and regulatory obligations.

Specific retention periods have not been stated in this draft because they must be approved against NIJA's actual legal obligations and production systems before release. The final policy should identify material retention periods or criteria where required by law.

## 7. Account and Data Deletion

Users can initiate deletion of their NIJA account from the app through **Settings → Privacy & Account → Delete Account**.

When a deletion request is accepted, NIJA will remove or de-identify personal data associated with the account where legally permitted. NIJA-held broker connection credentials and registered device/push tokens are intended to be removed as part of the active-account deletion process.

NIJA may retain limited records where reasonably necessary to comply with legal, tax, accounting, security, fraud-prevention, dispute-resolution, or regulatory obligations. Retained information should be restricted from ordinary product use and deleted or de-identified when the applicable retention obligation expires.

Deleting a NIJA account does not delete an account held directly with a brokerage, exchange, Apple, Google, payment provider, or other independent third party. Users must manage those accounts directly with the applicable provider.

Before Google Play submission, NIJA will publish a stable HTTPS page explaining how users can request account deletion outside the app.

## 8. Privacy Choices and Rights

Depending on where you live and subject to applicable exceptions, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, portability of, or deletion of personal information, or to object to or restrict certain processing.

Users may also:

- manage notification permissions through device settings;
- disconnect broker credentials through NIJA where the feature is available;
- delete their NIJA account through the in-app deletion flow;
- contact NIJA through the official support/privacy channel published on `nijaaitrading.com` for assistance with an authenticated or verified privacy request.

The final published policy must include any legally required jurisdiction-specific notices after counsel review.

## 9. Children

NIJA is not intended for children. The minimum permitted age and any financial-account eligibility requirements must be aligned with applicable law, connected broker requirements, and the final app-store age-rating answers before release.

## 10. International Processing

NIJA and its service providers may process information in jurisdictions other than the user's own. Any legally required transfer mechanisms or notices must be implemented based on NIJA's production vendors and target markets before release.

## 11. Third-Party Services

Brokerages, exchanges, Apple, Google, payment providers, and other third-party services operate under their own terms and privacy policies. NIJA does not control the independent data practices of those providers.

## 12. Changes to This Policy

NIJA may update this Privacy Policy as the service, legal requirements, or data practices change. The effective date will be updated when a revised policy is published. Material changes will be communicated when required by applicable law.

## 13. Contact

The final published version will identify NIJA's verified legal entity name, mailing address if legally required, and a monitored privacy contact. Until those details are verified, users should use the official contact/support channel published on `https://nijaaitrading.com`.
