Education and simulation are the safe default experience. Optional live functionality remains subject to user consent, broker connection, server-side risk controls, and execution-readiness checks.

NIJA does not guarantee profits, returns, income, trade fills, or trading success. Trading can result in partial or total loss.

## Store-review language guardrail

Avoid:

- guaranteed profit / guaranteed returns
- passive income promises
- get rich / financial freedom guarantees
- "AI makes money for you"
- risk-free trading
- 100% win rate
- claims of regulatory approval unless formally obtained and applicable

Prefer:

- education-first
- simulated trading
- broker-connected software
- user-controlled risk settings
- server-side safety and readiness checks
- no guarantees
- risk of loss
