# NIJA Mobile Counsel Review Packet

**Prepared:** August 27, 2026

This packet identifies the legal decisions counsel should resolve before NIJA publishes or submits the mobile application. It is not a legal opinion.

## Counsel should receive

- `mobile/PRIVACY_POLICY.md`
- `mobile/TERMS_OF_SERVICE.md`
- `mobile/DATA_INVENTORY.md`
- `mobile/STORE_PRIVACY_DISCLOSURES.md`
- `mobile/ACCOUNT_DELETION.md`
- `mobile/STORE_LISTING_MATERIALS.md`
- `mobile/REVIEWER_ACCESS.md`
- `mobile/APP_STORE_TRACK_A_READINESS.md`
- current subscription/pricing terms
- screenshots/video of the release candidate
- list of production vendors/processors and contracts/DPAs
- exact broker permissions and API credential architecture
- exact markets/jurisdictions where the app will be offered

## Questions requiring legal answers

1. What is NIJA's exact legal entity name and required public contact/mailing information?
2. How should NIJA characterize its education, simulation, automated execution, and broker-connected features without creating misleading financial-service claims?
3. Do any NIJA features implicate broker-dealer, investment-adviser, commodities/CTA/CPO, money-transmission, custody, signal-service, or comparable registration/licensing regimes in any target jurisdiction?
4. What disclosures and affirmative consents are required before live trading functionality becomes available?
5. What minimum age/eligibility language should NIJA use given app-store rules, broker requirements, and financial laws?
6. Which account, trading, consent, security, tax, payment, dispute, and audit records must be retained after deletion, and for how long?
7. Which records may be deleted immediately, de-identified, or retained in backups until normal expiry?
8. Is NIJA's account-deletion notice sufficiently clear about retained records and independent third-party brokerage accounts?
9. Which privacy laws apply based on NIJA's users, locations, revenue/volume thresholds, and data practices?
10. Does NIJA need jurisdiction-specific privacy notices, consumer request procedures, processor agreements, or international transfer mechanisms?
11. Are Apple/Google in-app purchase requirements compatible with NIJA's subscription model, web sales, Founders plans, and any external payment flows?
12. Are referral/affiliate relationships with brokerages required to be disclosed in-app, on the website, or in marketing materials?
13. What claims about historical/simulated performance may NIJA lawfully display, and what disclosures must accompany them?
14. What records and controls are required for complaints, incidents, cybersecurity events, and unauthorized trading allegations?
15. Are the Terms' limitation-of-liability, arbitration, governing-law, subscription, refund, termination, and risk provisions enforceable for target users?

## Counsel deliverables requested

- approved Privacy Policy;
- approved Terms of Service;
- approved trading/risk disclosure and consent language;
- approved account-deletion/retention language;
- approved subscription/refund/cancellation language;
- approved affiliate/referral disclosures;
- approved marketing-claims guardrails;
- jurisdiction launch restrictions, if any;
- written list of records NIJA must retain and required retention periods;
- written assessment of licenses/registrations that are required, not required, or require further analysis.

## Engineering hand-back

Counsel's conclusions should be converted into testable requirements: exact disclosure text/version, consent events to record, minimum age, prohibited jurisdictions, retention rules, deletion exceptions, required links, and marketing words/claims that are forbidden.
