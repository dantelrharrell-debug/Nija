# NIJA Founding 100 Customer Onboarding Handoff (implementation contract)

Purpose: every lead receives exactly one relevant next-step instruction; no step is marked completed based on a sent email or a visual-only website element.

## Authoritative progression
`applied -> email_ownership_verified -> approved -> subscription_recognized_or_checkout_pending -> registered -> member_identity_bound -> entitled -> dashboard_verified -> brokerage_selection -> broker_connected_readonly -> education_ready -> onboarding_complete`.

All transitions require a durable customer/application ID, event timestamp, idempotency key, and auditable source. Never infer `entitled` from a frontend field, a Stripe Payment Link redirect, or a customer-entered subscription ID. Webhooks must verify Stripe signatures and subscription ownership; legacy subscriptions require reviewed, one-to-one administrative reconciliation. Use the existing application and subscription rather than creating a second checkout. Status notices must not claim access until independently verified.

## Delivery requirements (email + signed-in dashboard)
For each transition: enqueue one stage-specific message to the **confirmed** address; track attempted/sent/provider-accepted/delivered/bounced separately. A provider-accepted email is not proof of inbox delivery. On hard bounce, suppress repeats, flag the admin, and provide a secure alternative contact route. Deduplicate by `nija_user_id + step + version`. Never email broker API credentials, passwords, reset tokens in plaintext, trading account numbers, or payment details. Only send direct site links from trusted NIJA domains. Support mobile layouts and member-specific stage hints.

1. **Application received** — “We received your NIJA application. Your next step is to confirm your email address through our secure verification process; approval and membership access are not yet active.”
2. **Email ownership verified; pending approval** — “Your email is confirmed. We are reviewing your application; we will notify you of approval and the next step. No additional application is needed.”
3. **Approved; no valid subscription** — “Your application is approved. Sign in or create a member account using your verified email and follow the official Founding 100 checkout once. Offer: 14 days free, then $50/month unless canceled. Your account will not be active until Stripe verifies the subscription.”
4. **Approved; existing valid subscription but no member login** — “Your existing subscription was located. Do not pay again. Create a secure member login using the verified email at https://nijaaitrading.com/register. Complete website email verification, then sign in at https://nijaaitrading.com/login. Your access will appear after NIJA securely links and verifies the existing subscription. Contact support if you encounter Pending.” Only send this when registration, verification delivery, and login are **production tested**.
5. **Member login ready; entitlement pending** — “Sign in at https://nijaaitrading.com/login. Your membership is being securely linked. Do not check out again; we will notify you when access is available.” Never promise dashboard access here.
6. **Entitlement + dashboard tested** — “Sign in at https://nijaaitrading.com/login and open your Member Dashboard. Review the trial date, education/paper-trading benefits, and Brokerage Setup. No further payment is needed for an existing valid trial.”
7. **Brokerage selection** — “From your authenticated dashboard, open Brokerage Setup. Select Kraken, Coinbase, Alpaca or OKX **only if the portal displays it as supported and available**. Review jurisdiction, eligibility, exchange requirements, permission scopes and minimums. You may use education/paper trading before connecting a live brokerage. Connecting does not enable live-money trading.”
8. **Secure broker connection tested and offered** — “Follow the broker-specific instructions **inside the verified secure NIJA portal**. Use OAuth if supported or provide trade/read-only exchange credentials exclusively through NIJA's verified encrypted server-side flow. Disable withdrawals, verify account ownership, and confirm successful permissions/connection. Never email, text, or upload API secrets to support/chat/forms.” DO NOT SEND this stage until the secure broker flow is production tested with authenticated user isolation; otherwise display “Broker connection temporarily unavailable; education/paper mode remains available.”
9. **Connected; education ready** — “Review the risk disclosures, position-sizing controls and paper-trading tutorial. Live-money execution requires separate explicit user authorization and system safeguards; it is not enabled by a connected account.”
10. **Completed** — only mark complete when login, verified entitlement, accessible purchased benefits, and customer-selected onboarding path are genuinely usable. Brokerage connection may be optional for education members; store explicit `broker_setup_skipped` rather than marking it connected.

## Exceptional paths
- Missing verification/recovery provider: block sign-in invitation as actionable, show admin `email_verification_delivery_not_configured`, and do not suggest login is ready.
- Payment Link legacy member: require original application verification, authenticated immutable member ID, independent Stripe customer/subscription/status/price checks, one-to-one unique binding, audit, then customer confirmation. No blind manual entitlement writes.
- Trial start: ensure **14 usable days from first verified access**, adjust original subscription through approved Stripe process and notify customer of the confirmed trial end; no duplicate charge.
- Connected-but-not-approved broker: explain selected broker is unavailable; offer other verified brokers or paper mode, not generic unsupported API-key collection.
- Follow-up: if no movement at each stage, send a single courteous reminder after 24h and 72h with an opt-out and support path; do not send repeated prompts to bounced/suppressed mailboxes. Admin escalation on 24h pending, all failures, and trial erosion.

## Acceptance checklist
Run a test customer through application -> verified mailbox -> approval -> single checkout -> signed registration -> verified entitlement -> secure dashboard -> broker selection -> optional read-only broker connection -> paper trading, and test an **existing subscription** case without a second charge. Confirm isolation (customer A cannot access B), reset and verification email delivery, idempotent webhook retries, bounced email handling, truthful stage reporting, revocation on canceled subscription, and 14 usable days. Do not publish security-sensitive changes on the strength of copy or a clean frontend build alone.
